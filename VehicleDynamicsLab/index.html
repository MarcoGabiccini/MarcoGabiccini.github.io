Vehicle Dynamics Lab

Interactive simulations for the course Dinamica dei Veicoli (Vehicle Dynamics), Master’s Degree in Vehicle Engineering, Università di Pisa.

Browser-based simulations for vehicle dynamics, from the steering geometry to the handling diagram. Click any card to open the simulator directly — no installation required. Many plots have a “?” button that explains what they show.

Steering geometry

steering law and steering mechanisms

Steering law and Ackermann

From the steering wheel to the angles of the two front wheels: the steering law with the Ackermann coefficient ε (Ackermann, parallel, anti-Ackermann), the velocity centre C and the resulting slip angles.

Steering mechanism

Four-bar linkage and rack-and-pinion, each sized by least squares to follow exact Ackermann or the steering law with ε = 1, 0, −1.

Double-track model structure

the equations, their loops, the axle characteristic

Structure of the double-track equations

The double-track equations as a Simulink-like block diagram: which equation computes which unknown, the algebraic loops and how they are solved, in four formulations. Dependency tree and the flow of one simulation step.

Sampling the axle characteristic

The axle characteristic point by point: for each front slip angle α1, the loop of the front-axle equations, the consistency of ãy and the sampled curve. Set-up effects and anti-roll bar can be switched on.

Graphical construction of the axle characteristic

The load-transfer line ΔZ1 = k1Y1 and the sum curves, without and with a front anti-roll bar, plus a sheet on how the bar works.

Double-track simulations

manoeuvres of the full model replayed in 3D, and its MAPs: Maps of Achievable Performance

Double-track simulations in 3D

Four manoeuvres of the double-track model replayed in 3D: a fast lap of the Circuito del Sele, a ramp steer up to the grip limit, an ISO double lane change and a Scandinavian flick. Tyre forces on the friction circle, load transfers and signals, in step with the animation.

MAPs of the double track

MAPs, the Maps of Achievable Performance proposed by Prof. Massimo Guiggiani: the steady states of the double-track model, sampled with slow ramp steers at constant speed, for three cars (understeering baseline, with downforce, oversteering). Four linked planes with level curves, the car in its body frame with the velocity centre, and the 3D viewer of every test, kept in step with the MAPs.

Handling diagram

branches, local understeer gradient, equilibria and stability

Axle characteristics

Front and rear axle characteristics with the Magic Formula: a cursor on the slip angle α and one on the lateral force F, with the cornering stiffnesses.

Handling diagram construction

The branches from the normalized axle characteristics: at each level ãy/g, the pairs of points on the two curves and their Δα = α1 − α2. Axle data as Magic Formula or as curve quantities (slope at the origin, peak, asymptote).

Local understeer gradient

Kγ along the branches, g Kγ = W1/Φ1 − W2/Φ2: where the vehicle understeers and where it oversteers, with the tangents to the axle characteristics and to the branches.

Equilibria and stability

Steady-state equilibria as intersections of the branches with the input line set by speed and steering angle, their stability, and the graphical reading of l/R.
